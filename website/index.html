01

Written from the first line.

Most systems are assembled. Zurbax was written: a C runtime, a graphics engine with its own typeface, a display server, a toolkit, an init system, a network stack with TLS, a sound server and an installer, all in one codebase that reads like one person wrote it.

  • BootYour PC's own logo stays on screen from power-on to desktop. No text, no flicker.
  • DesktopA top bar, a taskbar with live previews, and a launcher that fills the screen.
  • AppsFiles, a web browser, Mail, Music, Photos, Calendar, Notes, Writer, a terminal and more.
  • SettingsLight or dark, ten accent colours, ten wallpapers painted in code.

02

Built to play.

Steam runs with Proton for your Windows library. Controllers work everywhere, with vibration. Harbor puts Heroic, Lutris, Bottles, RetroArch and the rest one click away, and Zurbax ships its own 3D game, Glide.

  • AMD RADV and radeonsi
  • Intel ANV and iris
  • NVIDIA NVK on GeForce RTX 20 and newer
  • Firmware for every device, included

03

Two desktops, one machine.

The lock screen asks which one you want today. Zurbax's own desktop, or GNOME 51 built from GNOME's sources, with the Zurbax taskbar, the same wallpapers and your same files. Both run on Zurbax's own system services.

  • GNOMEFiles, Settings, Software, Calendar, Maps, Weather, Clocks, Text Editor, Console and the rest of GNOME's core apps.
  • FlathubThousands of Linux apps, each in its own sandbox, from Harbor or GNOME Software.
  • PrivateNo account to sign in, no telemetry, no ads. Your files stay on your disk.
